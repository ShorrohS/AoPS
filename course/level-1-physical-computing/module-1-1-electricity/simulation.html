💧 Water analogy:
🔋
Voltage = Water Pressure
The pump that pushes water (electrons) through the pipe. Higher pressure → more flow.
🌊
Current = Flow Rate
How many litres (electrons) pass per second. Determined by pressure ÷ resistance.
🚧
Resistance = Pipe Narrowness
A narrow pipe (high resistance) reduces flow for the same pressure.
