Real-world context
Water analogy
🔋
Voltage = Pressure
The pump pushing electrons through the pipe. Higher pressure → more flow.
🌊
Current = Flow Rate
Electrons passing per second. Determined by pressure ÷ resistance.
🚧
Resistance = Pipe Width
A narrow pipe reduces flow for the same pressure.
