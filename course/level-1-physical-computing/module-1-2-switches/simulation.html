Control coil and output contacts are in completely separate circuits. A 5mA signal can switch a 10A load — that's why relays are still used in cars and industrial equipment today.
Self-latching relay — once SET, the relay holds its own state through its own contacts, even after the SET button is released. This is the first 1-bit memory cell.
