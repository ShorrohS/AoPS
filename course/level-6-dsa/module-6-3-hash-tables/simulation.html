Linear probing: on collision, probe the next slot. When searching, follow the same probe sequence until you find the key or an empty slot. Deletion uses tombstones to avoid breaking probe chains.

Separate chaining: each bucket holds a linked list. All keys that hash to the same slot form a chain. Lookup scans the chain at the hash index. Works well even at high load factors (unlike open addressing).

Why DJB2? The multiplier 33 (prime-like) and XOR combine bytes in a way that makes similar strings produce very different hashes. Sum-only functions are vulnerable: "abc" and "bca" hash the same.
