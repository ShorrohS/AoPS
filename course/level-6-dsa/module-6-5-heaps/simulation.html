Floyd's algorithm builds a heap in O(n). Rather than inserting elements one by one (O(n log n)), it starts from the last non-leaf and sifts each internal node down. Most nodes are near the bottom and require few sifts.

A priority queue always gives you the highest-priority item next. Implemented with a heap: insert in O(log n), extract-max in O(log n), peek in O(1). Used in Dijkstra's algorithm, A*, task scheduling, and event simulation.
