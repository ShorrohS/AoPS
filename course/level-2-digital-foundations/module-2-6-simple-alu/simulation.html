C vs V — the key distinction
C (Carry) = unsigned overflow. Result exceeded 15 (for 4 bits). Detected as carry-out of MSB adder.

V (Overflow) = signed overflow. Two positives summed to negative, or two negatives summed to positive. Detected as: Cout XOR Cin of MSB.
