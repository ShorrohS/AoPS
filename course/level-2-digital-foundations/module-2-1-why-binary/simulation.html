Binary advantage
At every gate output, the binary signal is regenerated to 0V or 5V — noise resets to zero. Analog signals accumulate noise through every stage. This is why digital audio copies are perfect; analog tape degrades with every dub.
5V CMOS logic thresholds. Any input above 3.5V is read as logic 1. Below 1.5V is logic 0. The "forbidden zone" in between must never appear at an output — the gate drives it to a clean extreme.
