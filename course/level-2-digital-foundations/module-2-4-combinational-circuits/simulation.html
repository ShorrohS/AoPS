Half Adder — adds two 1-bit numbers. Produces a Sum (A XOR B) and a Carry (A AND B). Called "half" because it cannot accept a carry-in from a previous stage.
Inputs
Toggle A and B
A B
Outputs
0 Sum = A XOR B
0 Carry = A AND B
Boolean: Sum = A ⊕ B  ·  Carry = A · B
Implementation: 1 XOR gate + 1 AND gate

Full Adder — adds three 1-bit inputs: A, B, and Carry-In. Produces Sum and Carry-Out. Chain these to build multi-bit adders. Implementation: two half adders + one OR gate.
Inputs
Toggle A, B, Cin

0 Cout = (A·B) OR (Cin·(A XOR B))
Internals:
HA1: s1 = A ⊕ B, c1 = A·B
HA2: Sum = s1 ⊕ Cin, c2 = s1·Cin
Cout = c1 OR c2
