Embeddings map discrete tokens to continuous vectors. Words with similar meanings land near each other in vector space. Famous result: king − man + woman ≈ queen. The geometry of the space encodes semantic relationships.

Cosine similarity measures the angle between two vectors. It ignores magnitude — only direction matters. Score of 1 = identical direction. Score of 0 = orthogonal (unrelated). Score of −1 = opposite. It's the standard metric for comparing text embeddings.
