A bank transfer must be atomic — both debits and credits happen, or neither does. Without a transaction, a crash between operations leaves money lost. With ACID, the database recovers to a consistent state.

> Atomicity: both operations commit together, or neither does. The total always stays £1,500.
Write-Ahead Log: write changes to the log before applying to data pages. On crash recovery, replay committed log entries (REDO) and undo uncommitted ones. Recovery is O(log size), not O(disk size).

> WAL ensures durability: committed data survives crashes. Only the log needs to be flushed on commit.
