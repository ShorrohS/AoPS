Why B+ tree wins for ranges
Hash index: cannot answer range queries — keys are scattered randomly in hash buckets.

B+ tree: leaf nodes are sorted and linked. Range scan = 1 tree traversal + sequential leaf scan.

Full scan: read every row, filter at application layer.
