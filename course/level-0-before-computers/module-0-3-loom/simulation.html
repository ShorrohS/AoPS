Module 0.3 — Jacquard Loom Simulator

Program a punch card chain. The loom executes it — hole by hole, row by row.

How to use: Each row is one punch card. Each column is one warp thread. Click a cell to punch a hole (filled = thread UP). Leave it empty (thread stays DOWN). Press Weave — the loom reads your cards in sequence and builds the fabric.

The card chain is your program. The loom is the machine. They are separate.
