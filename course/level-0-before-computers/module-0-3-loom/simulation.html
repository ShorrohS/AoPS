🧵 Module 0.3 — Jacquard Loom Simulator

Program a punch card chain. The loom executes it — hole by hole, row by row.

How to use: Each row is one punch card. Each column is one warp thread. Click a cell to punch a hole (thread goes UP — shown in gold) or leave it empty (thread stays DOWN). When you press Weave, the loom reads your cards in sequence and builds the fabric.

The card chain is your program. The loom is the machine. They are separate.
