💡 The Real Insight
The speedup wasn't just convenient — it changed what was possible. Results in 6 weeks meant the data could actually influence policy decisions in the same Congress that commissioned the census. Results in 8 years were history, not intelligence.

This is why automation matters: it doesn't just do the same thing faster. It makes entirely new things possible.
