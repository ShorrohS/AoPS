# Level 0 — Before Computers: The Human Problem

> *Before there was a machine, there was a desperate need.*

---

## What This Level Is About

Computing didn't emerge from curiosity alone. It was forced into existence by real human problems that were simply too large, too repetitive, and too error-prone for unaided human minds to handle.

This level traces that journey — from counting beans on an abacus to punching holes in cards to tell a machine what to do. Every invention you'll meet here was born from frustration with the previous solution.

By the end of Level 0, you will understand:
- **Why** computation had to be mechanized
- **How** mechanical and electromechanical devices encoded and processed information
- **What** a "stored instruction" means and why it's the idea that made modern computers possible
- **How** punch cards bridged the gap between mechanical and digital computing
