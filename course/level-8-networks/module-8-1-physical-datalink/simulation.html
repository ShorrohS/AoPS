Manchester encoding: each bit includes a voltage transition. 1 = low→high (rising edge). 0 = high→low (falling edge). Every bit period has a transition, allowing the receiver to synchronise its clock even with long runs of the same bit.

Each pair of half-periods = one bit.
First half HIGH→second half LOW = 0
First half LOW→second half HIGH = 1
