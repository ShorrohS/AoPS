TLS 1.3 completes in 1 Round Trip (1-RTT). The client sends its key share in the first message. The server responds with its key share, certificate, and encrypted data simultaneously. Data flows immediately after the server's first response.

A certificate chain establishes trust from a root CA. Your browser trusts ~150 root CAs. Each root CA signs intermediate CAs; intermediate CAs sign server certificates. Verify each signature in the chain up to a trusted root.
