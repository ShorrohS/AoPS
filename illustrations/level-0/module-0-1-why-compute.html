Type C — Concept Diagram FIG 0.1-B What Is Computation? — Symbol, Rule, Manipulation
Computation is substrate-independent. The medium changes — beads, gears, voltage — but the abstract structure is identical: a symbol has a state, a rule specifies a transformation, a new symbol emerges.
Type D — Process / Timeline FIG 0.1-C Evolution of Human Computation — From Tally Sticks to Transistors
Computation timeline from 2400 BC to 1947. Five eras of substrate: manual aids, mechanical calculators, analytical engines, electro-mechanical tabulators, and electronic computers. The abstract concept of symbol manipulation is constant throughout.
